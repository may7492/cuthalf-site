Privacy Policy

cuthalf · last updated 2 September 2026

cuthalf is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. This policy explains what that means in practice.

What the app collects

Nothing. cuthalf does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind in this version.

What the app stores

Your progress — best score, coins, the items you own, your daily missions and streak, and your settings — is written to a file inside the app's own storage on your device. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. cuthalf never sees your name, your payment details, or your Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase succeeded. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

Because the app collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either. It is not directed at children under 13, and it has no chat, no social features, and no user content.

Changes

A future version of cuthalf may show advertising. Advertising uses device identifiers, so if that happens this policy will be updated to say exactly what is collected, and the App Store privacy labels will be updated alongside it, before that version ships. This page always describes the version currently on the App Store.
