cuthalf

Something wrong, or just want to ask? Write to marbelscreator@gmail.com and say which device and which iOS version you are on — that is usually enough to find the problem.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on the device, not to an account, so deleting the app deletes it and a new phone starts fresh. There is no way to recover it from our side, because we never had a copy.

I bought something and did not get it

Open the shop and use Restore Purchases. That asks Apple what this Apple Account owns and re-grants it. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. The short version: the game collects nothing and everything it saves stays on your device.
